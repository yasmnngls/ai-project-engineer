# Personas

Write the people who appear in requirements. Two is enough for almost every MVP. Do not invent a buyer, an admin, and a support agent unless the PRD gives them a job.

```markdown
# Personas

## P1 {Name}

- Role:
- Job: {the job this product is hired for}
- Context: {where they are and what they have with them}
- Success: {how they know they are done}

## P2 {Name}

- Role:
- Job:
- Context:
- Success:
```

`P1` is the primary user from the ask gate. Later personas only exist if an `in` requirement names them.
