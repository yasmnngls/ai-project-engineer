# Personas

## P1 Field technician

- Role: Technician who visits equipment sites.
- Job: Leave a visit record before driving away. [stated]
- Context: Standing at the site with a phone and a few minutes.
- Success: The visit is submitted, and the office can read it without calling.

## P2 Office reader

- Role: Office staff who need the record of a finished visit.
- Job: Read the submitted visit without reconstructing it from texts. [stated]
- Context: At a desk, after the technician has left or while they are still on site.
- Success: The visit shows the site, checklist, name, note, and submit time, and it cannot be changed by accident.
