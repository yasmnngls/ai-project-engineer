# Brief: Site Log

## Problem

A technician leaves a site and the office gets a phone call or a half-written text. The visit record is late, incomplete, or stuck in a personal notes app. [stated]

## User

A field technician at an equipment site, in the minutes before they drive away, who has to leave a record the office can read. [stated]

## Promise

The office can open a submitted visit and see the site, the checklist, the technician's name, the note, and the time it was submitted. [stated]

## MVP

A phone web app with one shared team passphrase. A technician can add a site, start a visit, complete that site's checklist, type their name and a note, and submit. Office staff can read submitted visits. No accounts, no photos, no offline queue. [stated]

## Non-goals

Photo attachments, offline submit, user accounts, comments on a visit, customer signatures, invoicing, and a native app.
